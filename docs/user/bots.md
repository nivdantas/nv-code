# Orchestrator

NV-CODE creates one Orchestrator for each environment. Open it from the top of the web or desktop sidebar. When several environments are connected, each row shows its host. On mobile, open the Orchestrator thread in **No project**.

Ask the Orchestrator to coordinate work across projects. It picks a provider and model for each task and launches a worker in the right project. Its panel shows its workers, standing instructions, and notes. You can edit the instructions and notes there.

When one of its local workers finishes or fails, NV-CODE saves a short note with the outcome and a link to the worker. The notes panel shows your curated notes and the 20 most recent worker notes. The Orchestrator can search older notes and past threads, and follow up when a worker needs input. To follow work on another environment, open that environment's Orchestrator or ask for a status check by thread link.

The Orchestrator stays in **No project** and stays active. Use `/clear` for a fresh conversation. Its instructions and notes live on the host, so they survive compaction, restarts, and switching devices.
